Licensing Model Overview

The software is offered under a commercial licensing model designed for
enterprise and white-label use.

Licenses are granted on a per-customer basis and are available under
two alternative pricing models:

1. Annual Subscription
2. Fixed-Term Perpetual License (30 months)

The annual subscription provides the right to use, modify, brand, and
commercially distribute the software, including as a white-label solution,
for the duration of the subscription term.

The fixed-term perpetual license grants the same rights for a one-time
fee, valid for a period of thirty (30) months from the effective date.
At the end of the term, the license may be renewed or converted to an
annual subscription.

Both license models allow:
- proprietary and closed-source use
- full white-label branding
- commercial distribution to end customers
- internal modification and integration

Support, maintenance, updates, and service-level agreements (SLAs) may
be included as part of a subscription or offered separately, subject to
commercial agreement.
